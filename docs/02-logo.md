# 02 · Logo

<p align="center"><img src="../brand/logo/ciao-logo.png" width="320" alt="Ciao logo"></p>

## Construction

A hand-lettered script wordmark — **ciao** — with a five-point star replacing the dot of the *i* and a long swash that underlines the word. White letterforms carry a heavy black outline, so the mark holds on any of the three menu colours and on photography.

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| Script wordmark | personality, warmth, "said out loud" feeling |
| Star (the *i* dot) | the brand's recurring symbol — reused as the Ciao Selection mark, backdrop star and sparkles |
| Swash | movement; it points forward, so the logo always sits at the start (left) of a layout |
| Black keyline | contrast on red, blue, brown and dark backgrounds |

Master file: [`brand/logo/ciao-logo.png`](../brand/logo/ciao-logo.png) — 1200 × 758 px, transparent background (RGBA).

## Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the **height of the star** on every side. Nothing — text, edges, other graphics — enters that area.

## Minimum size

| Medium | Minimum width |
|---|---|
| Screens (1080p) | 180 px (used at 118–146 px height on the boards) |
| Print | 30 mm |
| Favicon / app | use the star alone, min. 32 px |

## Approved backgrounds

| Background | Use |
|---|---|
| Ciao Red `#F54021` | hot board, intro — primary |
| Ice Blue `#1670B5` | cold board |
| Cocoa `#7A3E26` | dessert board |
| Espresso `#15110F` / `#1A1210` | dark surfaces, price list title bars |

## Placement on the boards

- Always top-left in the header band, vertically centred.
- Intro: centred, 820 px wide, animated in with a pop (see [06 · Motion](06-motion.md)).

## Misuse

- Do not recolour the letters or remove the black keyline.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate (except the intro pop) or add drop shadows.
- Do not re-typeset "ciao" in a font.
- Do not place it on busy photography without the keyline being clearly visible.
- Do not separate the star from the wordmark, except when the star is used alone as the brand mark.
