## Sizes

| Name | Meaning | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **UNICO** | single size | hot only — espresso bar, Turkish coffee, teas |

Replaced the older *Tek Boy / Küçük / Büyük*. The Italian names match the brand and read well in both Turkish and English.

Drinks that exist only at Ciao — named and designed for the brand by Alican Yıldırım.

| Drink | Hot / Cold | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Tarantino** | both | taro + espresso — a bold, violet cult classic; latte-art star on top |
| **Pistachio White Hot** | hot | white chocolate, pistachio cream, whipped cream, Antep pistachio |
| **Ruby** | hot | strawberry purée, espresso, vanilla — ruby-coloured |
| **Cincirella** | hot | pumpkin and espresso — the autumn "Cinderella" |
| **Salted Karamel Chocolate** | hot | dark chocolate, salted caramel, cocoa |
| **Kori Kohi** | cold | cold-brew ice cubes in vanilla milk — it gets *stronger* as it melts (Japanese *kōri kōhī*, iced coffee) |
| **Berry Hibiscus Cooler** | cold | hibiscus, forest fruit, lemon, soda, mint |
| **Cool Lime Refresha** | cold | lime, mint |

## Desserts — a cinema, stage and art programme

Each dessert has a stage name that points straight at what it is — colour, ingredient or shape — and keeps its original name underneath.

| Stage name | Product | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Monet'nin Bahçesi | carrot cake | a garden of carrots, walnuts and spice |
| Moulin Rouge | red-fruit cheesecake | red, rich, theatrical |
| Frida | berry hibiscus cake | flowers and bold colour |
| Mavi Dönem | blueberry cake | Picasso's Blue Period |
| Altın Lotus | Lotus Biscoff cake | the golden lotus — caramelised Lotus, styled like a film award |
| Çikolata Fabrikası | Nutella cookie cake | *Charlie and the Chocolate Factory* — cookie dough and Nutella |
| Copacabana | Cocostar cake | coconut cream — the tropical show stage |
| Gaudí | mosaic cake | it *is* a mosaic |
| Noir | brownie | dark, intense — film noir |
| Kuğu Gölü | profiterole | choux "swans" — Swan Lake |
| Chocolat | chocolate cookie | the chocolatier film *Chocolat* — dark and milk chocolate chips |
| Roma Tatili | ice cream cup, 4 scoops | *Roman Holiday* — gelato on the steps |
| Pulp Fetta | slice of pizza | *fetta* = slice; a nod to Tarantino — **coming soon** |
| Il Padrino | whole pizza | *The Godfather* in Italian — **coming soon** |

## Ciao Duetto (dessert × coffee)

*Duetto* is Italian for duet: the dessert and the coffee share the stage.

Noir + Espresso · Altın Lotus + Lotus Latte · Copacabana + Türk Kahvesi · Roma Tatili + Espresso (affogato).
